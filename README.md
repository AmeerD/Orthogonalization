# Testing hypotheses via orthogonalization

Code to reproduce all figures from "Testing hypotheses via orthogonalization" (Dharamshi, Zou, and Witten, 2026).

The "Two Sample", "Clustering", and "Changepoint" folders contain the code to reproduce the figures in Section 5.1, Section 5.2, and Supplement C.2, respectively. The "Data Analysis" folder contains the code to reproduce the analysis in Section 6.

This material is based upon research supported in part by the Office of Naval Research of the United States, the National Science Foundation of the United States, the National Institutes of Health of the United States, and the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada.
